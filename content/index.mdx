---
title: Overview
description: What the Clackxx Project is.
---

The Clackxx Project recreates the "Clacks", the signalling towers from Terry Pratchett's Discworld novels, as a network of small robots. Each robot shows messages on a grid of shutters and lights, while the messages themselves travel between robots by wireless radio.

It's a long-running student project across Robotics, Digital Technologies, Networking and Cyber Security. The system starts out simple on purpose, and each year's students improve on what the last group built.
